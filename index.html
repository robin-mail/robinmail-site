Studio notes

Software that finds what's hard to find.

Robin Mail is an independent software studio. We build finder tools for law firms and other businesses that rely on public information scattered across the internet: reports, notices and records that are easy to miss and slow to track down.

AI is part of how we design, build and test our work.

How a finder works
  1. 01Name the thing you keep checking forA kind of report, notice or change that matters to your work.
  2. 02Watch the places it tends to show upNews, public posts, logs and records, checked continuously.
  3. 03Bring back useful matchesA short brief for each one, with the original source attached for review.
Where we start

Our first finders are built for West Virginia. The same approach can be set up for other states, and for any kind of public information a business needs to keep track of.

A Robin Mail finder

Firewatch

Firewatch gathers public reports of potentially severe structure fires in West Virginia, shows why each one was flagged, and keeps the original source attached so a person can check it.

Why it exists

Serious fires get reported in scattered places.

A news story here, a fire department post there, a line in a public log. For a business that needs to know about these fires quickly, they're easy to miss and slow to piece together. Firewatch puts them in one place and says plainly why each one stood out.

How it works

  1. 01Watch selected public sourcesLocal news, public fire department posts and public incident logs.
  2. 02Flag signs of substantial damageWords and details that suggest a structure was badly damaged or lost.
  3. 03Write a short briefWhat was reported, why it was flagged and what's still unknown, with the source linked for review.

Possible severe damage

Reported
House fire. Home described as a total loss.
Why flagged
A residential structure and the words "total loss" in the same report.
Still unknown
Exact address, cause, and whether other sources agree.
For review
Open the source and confirm the details before acting.

Where it stands

In development.

Firewatch is not an emergency alert or dispatch service. A flag is a lead for a person to check, not confirmation that a fire happened. This page describes the tool; it doesn't show live data.
